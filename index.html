🌺 Why We Celebrate 9 Days

Navratri • Nine nights of Shakti

Navratri means “nine nights” and is a major Hindu festival dedicated to the Divine Mother. During Sharad Navratri, devotees worship Goddess Durga and her forms, with traditions varying by region and community.

The nine days are traditionally associated with the Navadurga — Shailaputri, Brahmacharini, Chandraghanta, Kushmanda, Skandamata, Katyayani, Kalaratri, Mahagauri and Siddhidatri. The festival expresses the victory of dharma and good over evil and is also a period of prayer, fasting, reflection and renewal.

🌺 About Bhavani Mata

Shakti • Protection • Courage • Devotion

🙏 Maa Bhavani

Bhavani is a revered name and form of the Divine Mother associated with Durga/Shakti. In many traditions she represents courage, protection, strength and compassionate motherhood.
